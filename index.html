EMUNAH

אֱמוּנָה

Emunah (אֱמוּנָה) is a Hebrew word often translated as faithfulness, steadfastness or fidelity. But its meaning extends beyond simply believing. It speaks of firmness, constancy, enduring trust and faith expressed through faithfulness.

Throughout Scripture, emunah describes a faithfulness that remains steadfast. In Deuteronomy 32:4, God is described as the faithful God, while Habakkuk 2:4 connects faith with the way the righteous live.

It is from this understanding of faith and faithfulness that Emunah, our Faith Fellowship Collective, takes its name.

Who We Are

We are a Faith Fellowship Collective of people from different churches, denominations, backgrounds and professions, united by our identity in Christ.

Our unity goes beyond a shared love for worship, music or creativity. It is rooted in who we are in Christ and the life we are called to live through Him.

We believe that in Christ, we are chosen and set apart for His purpose, distinguished by His presence and satisfied in His sufficiency. We are recipients of His love, partakers of His mercy and witnesses to His glory.

This identity shapes how we live, how we gather and how we relate to one another. Rooted in Scripture and led by the Holy Spirit, we seek to live in obedience to the God whose faithfulness never fails.

About Emunah

Our Beginning

Emunah began with a simple instruction: to gather for a night of worship.

We don’t have a complete picture of what Emunah will become or where the journey will lead. What we have is an instruction, and the conviction to respond in faith. What began as an act of obedience became the foundation of a Faith Fellowship Collective, trusting God to reveal the direction as we follow His lead.

אֱמוּנָה

The Heart of Emunah

Our name comes from the Hebrew word emunah, which speaks of steadfast trust, faithfulness and enduring commitment. At the heart of our collective is the understanding that God’s faithfulness is constant, even when our understanding is incomplete.

We believe faith is more than what we profess. It is reflected in how we live, how we obey and how we respond to the God who remains faithful.

Our Identity

We are united by our identity in Christ, beyond churches, denominations, backgrounds and professions. We believe we are chosen, set apart for His purpose, distinguished by His presence and satisfied in His sufficiency.

  • Chosen
  • Set apartfor His purpose
  • Distinguishedby His presence
  • Satisfiedin His sufficiency

This understanding shapes our fellowship, our worship and our commitment to growing together in Christ.

The Journey Ahead

Emunah is not defined by any single gathering or programme. We envision a collective where faith is lived, fellowship is nurtured and people are encouraged to grow in their relationship with God and one another.

We don’t claim to know every expression Emunah will take. We simply remain willing to follow where God leads, trusting in His faithfulness every step of the way.

Who Emunah Welcomes

Emunah welcomes Christians from all churches, denominations and backgrounds to be part of the collective. Our gatherings are also open to those who are curious about faith, seeking to know God or simply wish to experience Christian fellowship.
